DMCA & Content Disclaimer

Last Updated: September 8, 2026 • Effective Immediately

Server Storage Statement

Hakuna TV does not host, upload, broadcast, or store video media files, streams, or copyrighted content on our own web servers. Our services function solely as an indexing middleware protocol linking client applications to publicly accessible streaming URLs.

1. DMCA Compliance Overview

Hakuna TV respects the intellectual property rights of all copyright holders and operates in full compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). It is our strict policy to respond promptly to clear, formal notices of alleged copyright infringement.

2. How to File a Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative thereof and believe that any content linked through our indexing directory infringes upon your copyright, please submit a written notification to our Designated Copyright Agent including the following:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or person authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (e.g., specific title and registration information).
  • Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, including the specific stream link or M3U entry to be removed.
  • Your contact information, including your full legal name, physical mailing address, telephone number, and official corporate email address.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

3. Designated DMCA Agent Contact

Please direct all official copyright infringement notices to our designated compliance desk:

  • Email: dmca@hakuna4ktv.shop
  • Response Window: Valid, properly documented DMCA requests are reviewed and processed within 24 to 48 business hours.
