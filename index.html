Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and streaming with Hakuna TV.

What is IPTV and how does Hakuna TV work?

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers television programming and video content directly over your broadband connection instead of conventional satellite or cable lines. After choosing a plan, our automated system generates your dedicated M3U playlist link and Xtream Codes API credentials. You simply paste these into your favorite player app (e.g. TiviMate, IPTV Smarters) and start streaming instantly.

How fast does my internet connection need to be for 4K streaming?

For standard definition (SD), 10 Mbps is adequate. For Full HD 1080p, we recommend at least 25 Mbps. For uncompressed 4K Ultra HD and 60FPS live sports broadcasts, a stable connection of 35–50 Mbps or higher ensures zero buffering.

How long does it take to activate my subscription after payment?

Activation is immediate and fully automated. In less than 2 minutes following checkout, your setup details and credentials will arrive in your email inbox. If you provide your WhatsApp number during checkout, you will also receive a instant backup confirmation.

Can I use my subscription on multiple devices simultaneously?

You can configure your subscription credentials on as many devices as you own (Living Room TV, Bedroom Firestick, Smartphone, Tablet). Standard plans support 1 active stream at a time. If you require concurrent multi-room streaming, you can choose a multi-connection add-on during checkout.

Which IPTV applications do you recommend?

For Android and Fire TV Stick, we strongly recommend TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro for the fastest channel switching and cleanest guide navigation. For Samsung and LG Smart TVs, IBO Player or Smart IPTV are top performers. For Apple iOS and Apple TV, IPTV Smarters or GSE Smart IPTV work flawlessly.

What is your refund policy?

We stand behind the quality and stability of our servers. We offer an unconditional 7-day money-back guarantee. If you encounter any technical difficulty our support team cannot resolve, simply contact our 24/7 support desk for a full refund.

Do I need a VPN to use Hakuna TV?

A VPN is not required because all streams are encrypted via secure SSL tunnels. However, if your Internet Service Provider actively throttles video streaming traffic during peak hours or live sports matches, using a reliable VPN (such as Surfshark, NordVPN, or ExpressVPN) will bypass ISP throttling.

Are there recurring subscriptions or hidden cancellation fees?

Absolutely none. We operate on a 100% prepaid basis. When your selected duration concludes, your service simply pauses unless you deliberately choose to renew. You will never be billed automatically without your explicit consent.
