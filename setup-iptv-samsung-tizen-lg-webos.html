How to Set Up IPTV on Samsung Tizen & LG webOS Smart TVs

📅 Published: September 24, 2026 ✍️ Author: Hakuna TV ⏱️ 5 Min Read

How to Set Up IPTV on Samsung Tizen & LG webOS Smart TVs

Why Choose IPTV on a Smart TV?

IPTV turns your television into a flexible, on‑demand entertainment hub without the need for external set‑top boxes. By using the native operating systems—Samsung’s Tizen and LG’s webOS—you keep the remote control simple, enjoy faster launch times, and retain full access to the TV’s picture‑quality settings.

Prerequisites

  • A stable broadband connection (minimum 10 Mbps for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K).
  • A valid IPTV subscription that provides an M3U playlist URL or an Xtream Codes API.
  • The TV’s firmware updated to the latest version.
  • A wireless or wired network connection that the TV can access.
  • The remote control that came with the TV (or a compatible universal remote).

Installing IPTV on Samsung Tizen

  1. Turn on the TV and press the Home button on the remote.
  2. Select Apps from the bottom navigation bar.
  3. In the Apps screen, click the magnifying glass icon and type "Smart IPTV" or "IPTV Smarters Pro". Both are approved by Samsung’s store.
  4. Choose the app, then click Install. Wait for the download to finish (usually under a minute on a 20 Mbps line).
  5. After installation, open the app from My Apps.
    • If the app asks for a license key, enter the key provided by your IPTV provider.
  6. Navigate to the Settings or Playlist section inside the app.
    • Select Add Playlist and choose URL as the source.
    • Paste your M3U URL exactly as given, then confirm.
  7. Return to the main screen of the app. Your channel list should appear within a few seconds.
    Tip: If the list is empty, double‑check that the URL includes the .m3u extension and that there are no extra spaces.
  8. Use the TV remote to navigate channels, enable subtitles, or adjust the aspect ratio via the app’s built‑in settings.

Installing IPTV on LG webOS

  1. Press the Home button on your LG remote and select LG Content Store.
  2. Choose the Apps & Services tab, then use the search field to type "IPTV Smarters Pro" or "SS IPTV".
  3. Click Install on the app tile. The download size is typically under 10 MB.
  4. Open the newly installed app from the Home Dashboard.
    • Some apps require you to log in with a username/password; use the credentials supplied by your provider.
  5. Inside the app, go to Playlist Management → Add New Playlist → URL.
    • Paste the M3U URL, then tap Save.
  6. After saving, the channel list will populate. If you see a loading spinner for more than 30 seconds, press the Refresh button.
    Note: LG webOS caches playlists for 24 hours. To force a refresh, delete the playlist and re‑add it.
  7. Use the remote’s directional pad to scroll through channels, and press the OK button to launch a stream.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

  • No channels appear after adding the playlist. Verify the URL is correct, includes .m3u, and that your provider’s server is online. Test the URL on a PC using VLC to confirm it works.
  • Buffering or frequent pauses. Reduce the video quality in the app’s settings (e.g., switch from 1080p to 720p). Ensure the TV is connected via Ethernet if Wi‑Fi signal is below 50 %.
  • Authentication error. Re‑enter the username/password or license key. Some providers lock an IP after multiple failed attempts; wait 10 minutes before retrying.
  • App crashes on launch. Clear the app cache: Settings → General → Storage → Select the IPTV app → Clear Cache. Then restart the TV.
  • Audio out of sync. Open the app’s audio settings and toggle the “Audio Sync” option, or adjust the TV’s sound delay in Settings → Sound → Advanced Audio.

Tips for Optimal Performance

  • Place the TV within 5 meters of the router or use a wired Ethernet adapter for the most stable stream.
  • Enable HDMI‑CEC on the TV so the remote can control playback without switching inputs.
  • Update the IPTV app monthly; developers often release patches that improve compatibility with new Tizen/webOS versions.
  • Disable other bandwidth‑heavy devices (gaming consoles, large downloads) while watching live TV.
  • Use the TV’s built‑in picture mode “Game” or “Cinema” for the best color accuracy when streaming 4K content.

FAQ

  • Do I need a separate set‑top box? No. Both Samsung Tizen and LG webOS can run IPTV apps natively, eliminating extra hardware.
  • Can I record IPTV streams? Recording is not supported by most official IPTV apps due to DRM restrictions. Some third‑party apps offer local recording, but check your provider’s terms of service.
  • Is a VPN required? A VPN can help bypass geo‑restrictions, but it may add latency. If you experience buffering, try disabling the VPN first.
  • What if my TV model is older than 2017? Older Tizen (pre‑2017) and webOS versions may not support the latest IPTV apps. In that case, consider using an Android TV box or a compatible external streaming device.
